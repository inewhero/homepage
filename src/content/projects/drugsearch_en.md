Medication cost often enters a clinical conversation before anyone has time to consult several websites or price tables. A quick reference can help staff give patients a concrete estimate and keep affordability visible when discussing early treatment options.

DrugSearch is a small desktop interface that queries public price information by drug name. It presents formulation, specification, listed price, manufacturer, notes, and source in one sortable table. The tool was built for fast use in the department, where a short search can support patient communication and routine psychiatric medication-cost planning.
