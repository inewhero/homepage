## About the study

Using data from 1,133 middle school students, the study combined latent profile analysis, network analysis, and machine learning to examine heterogeneity in smartphone addiction risk. It identified low-, moderate-, and high-risk profiles, then investigated how childhood neglect and materialism were organised within those groups and whether profile information improved prediction.
